motion graphics

motion principle

chart motion should explain structure. nodes enter in a short sequence, edges draw after their endpoints appear, and notes pop in as proof points. crisp and playful, not cinematic or slow.

orientshow the starting node before any edge so the reader has a stable anchor.
connectdraw the route only after both related surfaces are visible.
annotatenotes enter last, with a small pop — they explain rather than define the structure.
timing 80–140 ms between graph items
420–720 ms per pop
no looping unless the chart is explicitly live

rules for the next pass

these rules are the practical audit outcome. they keep the system apple-grade without erasing the portfolio’s own voice.

  • merge through tokensreplace the poster and project split with semantic surface roles: page, paper, inset, line, cool line, accent, ink, body.
  • keep one type voiceuse archivo as the identity face, but borrow apple restraint: lower weights inside components, shorter lines, no decorative labels.
  • make chrome deferuse glass only for navigation and floating chrome. content cards stay opaque enough to protect contrast.
  • let density varyshell sections can breathe; project panels can be compact. both should share the same material rules.
  • centralize chartsall future charts should consume the same chart system tokens and components, not hand-tuned class sets.
  • write lowercasevisible labels and headings should stay lowercase or sentence-like; no all caps, no title-case labels, no wide tracked badges.
